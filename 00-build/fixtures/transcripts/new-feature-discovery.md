# Customer discovery synthesis — team collaboration
Date: 2026-07-23 · Attendees: PM (you), Research (Malik), Sales eng (Tova), Eng lead (Dara)

**Malik:** I've now done nine interviews with accounts that churned between month two
and month four. There's a pattern I did not expect. Every single one of them activated
fine — they got through onboarding, they hit first value. They churned anyway.

**PM:** So this isn't an onboarding problem.

**Malik:** No. It's a second-user problem. In eight of the nine, exactly one person was
using the product. When that person went on leave, changed teams, or got busy, usage
went to zero and never came back. There's no team in the account, so there's no
resilience.

**Tova:** Sales sees the mirror image. Deals stall when the champion can't show it to
their team. We have no invite flow worth the name — you can add a seat but there's no
shared workspace, no way to hand a piece of work to someone else.

**Malik:** Two of them said, almost word for word, "I couldn't get anyone else to care
because there was nothing for them to do in it."

**Dara:** That's a whole new surface, not a tweak. Shared workspaces, roles and
permissions, assignment, some notion of activity a second person can respond to. That's
a quarter of work minimum, and it touches the data model.

**PM:** And it's genuinely a different problem statement from Northstar. Northstar is
"get one person to value fast." This is "get a team to depend on it." Different user,
different metric, different scope. I don't want to bolt this onto the onboarding PRD.

**Malik:** Agreed — and the retention data supports treating it as its own bet. The
month-two-to-four cliff is the single biggest leak in the funnel right now.

**Tova:** Worth noting a competitor already has a version of this. Whatever we write
should be checked against what's actually in market.

**PM:** Then let's start a new PRD for team collaboration and get the market read into
it before we commit any scope.
