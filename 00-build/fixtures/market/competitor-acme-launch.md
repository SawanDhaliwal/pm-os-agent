# Acme Product Blog — "Onboarding that finishes itself"
Published: 2026-07-18

Today we're rolling out AI-guided setup to all Acme plans. Instead of showing new users
a checklist of tasks to complete, Acme now completes most of them automatically:

- We read the user's connected data source on signup and pre-populate their first
  project, so the first screen is never empty.
- Remaining setup steps are done by an agent the user can watch and correct, rather
  than a list the user works through manually.
- Median time-to-first-value in our beta cohort fell from 22 minutes to 4 minutes.
- Activation (defined as completing a first meaningful action in week one) rose from
  41% to 63% in the beta cohort over eight weeks.

Two competitors now offer a manual guided checklist. We think the checklist itself is
the wrong abstraction: asking a new user to do configuration work is the problem, not
the sequencing of it.

Availability: all plans, all regions, effective immediately.
