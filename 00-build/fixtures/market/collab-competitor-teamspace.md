# Teamspace Inc — "Shared workspaces, now on every plan"
Published: 2026-07-19

Teamspace is moving shared workspaces out of Enterprise and into every paid tier,
including the $12 Starter plan.

What ships today:
- Shared workspaces with per-workspace membership, available on all paid plans.
- Three roles (owner / editor / viewer) with item-level assignment.
- An activity feed a second user can respond to, plus @mention notifications.
- A "hand off" action that transfers ownership of an item with its history intact.

Why we did it: our own data showed accounts with a single active user churning at
roughly three times the rate of accounts with two or more. Gating collaboration behind
Enterprise was, in effect, gating retention behind Enterprise.

Early results from the staged rollout: accounts reaching a second weekly active user
within 30 days rose from 19% to 34%. Logo churn in the month-two-to-four window fell
about 1.8 points over the same period.

We expect single-player-only products in this category to feel the difference within two
quarters.
