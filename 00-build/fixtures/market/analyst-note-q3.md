# Bellwether Research — Q3 2026 note: activation benchmarks in collaborative SaaS
Circulated: 2026-07-15 (licensed excerpt)

Median week-one activation across the 34 vendors we track is 44%, up two points
year-over-year. The top quartile sits at 61%.

Two observations from this quarter's data:

1. The gap between median and top-quartile activation is increasingly explained by how
   much configuration work the vendor removes, not by how well it is explained. Vendors
   that pre-populate initial state outperform vendors that guide users through creating
   it, holding onboarding UX quality constant.

2. Single-user accounts churn at roughly 3x the rate of accounts with two or more weekly
   active users by month four. We consider "second user acquisition" the most
   under-instrumented metric in the category.

We expect guided-checklist patterns to be table stakes rather than differentiating
within 12 months.
