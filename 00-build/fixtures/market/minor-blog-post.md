# SmallUX Weekly — "Say 'Got it', not 'Dismiss'"
Published: 2026-07-22

A short one this week. We ran a copy test across four onboarding tooltips and found that
labelling the dismissal action "Got it" rather than "Dismiss" lifted click-through on the
tooltip's primary action by about 1.5 percentage points (n=2,100, one product, one
locale, three weeks).

Caveat: this is a single product and a single locale, and the effect was inside the
confidence interval for two of the four tooltips. We would not generalise from it. It is
mostly interesting as a reminder that dismissal copy is doing more work than teams
assume.
